## <img src="https://fonts.gstatic.com/s/e/notoemoji/latest/1f44b/512.gif" width="30"/> About Me

> Hey, I'm **Haris**! An Electrical Engineer who loves turning sensors, signals and images into useful intelligence. I work across **biomechanics sensing, human motion monitoring, and medical image analysis**, and I'm always open to collaborating on research papers, interesting projects and PhD/MSc opportunities.

- 🎓 **Education:** B.Sc. Electrical Engineering, **Namal University Mianwali** (ranked **1st in cohort**, 2024)
- 🚆 **Currently:** Station Equipment Controller (CBTC, SCADA/HMI, Fire Alarm & Building Automation) on urban rail infrastructure
- 🔬 **Research:** Research Assistant / Computer Vision Engineer under **Dr. Ahmed Salim**
- 📚 **Publications:** IEEE and IET venues, with more accepted, submitted or under review
- 🎯 **Looking for:** MSc / PhD in Biomedical Engineering, Robotics and Intelligent Systems
- 🪪 **PEC Registered** Electrical Engineer (Pakistan Engineering Council)
